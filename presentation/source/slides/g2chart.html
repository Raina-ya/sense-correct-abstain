The gap-bound condition alone beats the full rule; the oracle error indicator barely beats the baseline. The gain comes from excluding large-gap units, not from detecting who benefits.

為什麼說 Gate 2 不是選擇性：光用 gap 上界條件就比完整規則好（0.596 vs 0.587），而 oracle 誤差指標幾乎跟基準一樣。所以增益來自排除大 gap 單位（那些單位本來就答對），而不是偵測出誰受益。
