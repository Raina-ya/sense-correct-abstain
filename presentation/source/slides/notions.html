Sensors and identifiability

Three notions to keep apart

(i) Zero efficient information

The ε-score lies in the closure of the nuisance tangent span — a first-order statement.

(ii) Local non-identification

I contains points other than ε₀ arbitrarily close to it.

(iii) Global non-identification

I is the whole parameter set.

(i) does not imply (ii)

So every non-identification claim here is proved by an explicit observational equivalence.

三個概念必須分開：零有效資訊、局部不可識別、全域不可識別。零資訊不代表不可識別——反例：N(ε³,1) 在 0 的 Fisher 資訊是零，但映射是單射。所以本文所有不可識別的結論都用明確的觀測等價構造來證明，而不是靠資訊為零。
