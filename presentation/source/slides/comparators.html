Always correct

Why allocation (ii) matters

Under a decision-flipping bias, fewer target shots lower the issue error (towards 1/2 rather than 1). Without (ii), an information-free policy could beat the comparator through allocation alone (P2).

Lfixed is the best of these, selected once on development data.
