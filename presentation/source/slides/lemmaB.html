A global bound on the gap shift

Lemma B · gap-shift bound

For all ε, φ ∈ ℝ, each tagged angle in exactly one gate. The constant ½ is sharp.

Proof idea

Integrate the gradient along θ + tδ; E(x) = cos²(x/2) attains ½.

Scope: global, no remainder; on the frozen populations the actual shift never exceeds 0.431 (Gate 2) or 0.213 (Gates 3–4) of ½B. It says nothing about the plug-in B̂ — a heuristic alarm threshold.

Lemma B：gap 偏移的全域上界，只依賴總角度和旋轉數，常數 1/2 是緊的。證明關鍵：沿任一角度，期望分數是 a+ρcos 的形式，且在 0 到 1 之間，所以振幅最多 1/2，導數最多 1/2。注意：這個界對插入估計值的 B̂ 沒有保證，B̂ 只是啟發式的警報門檻。
