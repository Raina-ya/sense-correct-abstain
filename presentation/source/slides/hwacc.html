Accuracy, and what the pilot does not show

Accuracy · biased

At zero injection the mean estimate was −0.0128 [−0.0166, −0.0086], triggering the prespecified bias flag; φ was overestimated by +0.0075 to +0.0094 at every point.

A native coherent phase on the pair — for example from the CZ gate or a static ZZ interaction, or crosstalk — is a possible cause that the pilot did not test.

What the pilot does not show

  • correction, decisions, or the adaptive policy
  • that hardware has an angle-proportional error
  • more than one processor, qubit pair and calibration window

An offset of about twice the precision threshold is comparable to the errors the grid injects (|ε| ≤ 0.04): recalibrating with uncorrected estimates could itself add error (not tested).

準確度：零注入時偏誤 −0.0128，觸發預先設定的偏誤旗標。CZ 或 ZZ 交互作用、串擾只能說是「可能的原因」，pilot 沒有檢驗。也要清楚說 pilot 沒有顯示什麼：沒測修正、決策或策略，也沒顯示硬體有角度成正比的誤差。偏移量大約是精確度門檻的兩倍，用未修正的估計去校準反而可能增加誤差。
