Execution plan

Processor

ibm_marrakesh (Heron), qubits 53 (target) and 54 (control): the pair with the smallest readout plus CZ error in the calibration snapshot.

Injected points

(0, 0), (±0.02, 0), (0.04, 0.01), (−0.04, −0.01); each estimate uses the frozen 18 × 135 = 2430 shots.

Replication and controls

8 blocks, each with all five points in seeded random order, a phase-blind negative control per point, and readout calibration: 134,600 shots.

Lock

Hashed lock committed about two minutes before the first job; execution gated on that hash. Approval recorded afterwards, no external timestamp: prespecified under an internal lock, not preregistered.
