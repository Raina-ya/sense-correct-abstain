Lemma · frame covariance

Hence the label-t circuit implements logical angles (1+ε)θ + s(t)φ, and at φ = 0 every label pattern yields the same outcome law.

Proof

No global phase appears; substitute and use s² = 1.

Consequence: the multiplicative part ε is label-blind; only φ is modulated by the label.

框架協變引理：Pauli 標籤夾住旋轉，等於把角度符號翻轉。證明一行：σZσ = sZ。後果非常關鍵：ε 對標籤是盲的，只有 φ 被標籤調制。所以在 φ=0 時，所有標籤給出同一個分佈——這是 Gate 1A 失敗的根源。
