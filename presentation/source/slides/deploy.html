Deployability boundary

The policy may use

  • runtime outcomes and recorded labels
  • pre-execution metadata
  • analytically known reference quantities
  • calibration whose shots are charged to N
  • development-locked constants

The policy may not use

  • the ideal target gap
  • the true (ε, φ)
  • the error-structure label
  • simulator-only response derivatives
  • confirmatory outcomes
可部署邊界：策略只能用真實執行時拿得到的東西。理想 gap、真實誤差參數、模擬器才有的導數都不能用。這保證結果對真實部署有意義。
