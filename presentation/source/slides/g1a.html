Retained labels cannot identify ε at φ = 0

Gate 1A · FAIL

Why

Lemma S1′, the Gibbs corollary and S1″: the profile maximiser is non-unique at the nine φ = 0 points of every design and every reading; the specification turns this into FAIL.

The frozen run’s I3, recovery and control entries were hard-coded, so the verdict rests on the analysis, not the report.

How not to read it

Post-lock: efficient information for ε at φ = 0 is zero (≤ 6.8 × 10⁻²¹ per shot), while the oracle information is 0.24–0.62 per shot.

Labels are informative when the response is known — this is not “RC labels cannot sense ε”.
