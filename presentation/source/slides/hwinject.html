Why the error is injected

Injected rather than native error

On IBM processors Z rotations are zero-duration updates of the control frame: there is no physical rotation whose angle could be miscalibrated.

So the pilot injects the compiled-angle error with known (ε, φ), which the estimator never sees.

What that changes

The pilot measures how precisely and how accurately P-amp recovers a known perturbation under real noise.

It says nothing about whether such an error occurs naturally.

IBM 的 Z 旋轉是虛擬的、零時長的框架更新，沒有可以被校準錯的物理旋轉。所以 pilot 是「注入」已知誤差，估計器看不到真值。這改變了估計目標：它測的是在真實雜訊下 P-amp 還原已知擾動的精確度和準確度，不代表這種誤差會自然發生。
