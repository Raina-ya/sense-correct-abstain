Error and compilation model

The P-amp sensor

Circuits

Control in |0⟩, target in |+⟩; six repetition and three echo circuits; measure ⟨X⟩ and ⟨Y⟩ on the target. 18 settings × 135 shots.

Estimator

Phases are linear in (ε, φ) with known coefficients; the estimate is their least-squares inversion.

Checked

A state-vector check of all 729 circuit–grid cases agrees with the phase model to 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁵, with zero control leakage (post-lock).

Sensor and target share one (ε, φ) by construction, so sensor–target transfer cannot fail in this model.

P-amp 感測器：在已知相位的參考比特上重複並 echo 被標記的旋轉，相位對 (ε, φ) 是線性的，用最小平方反解。729 個電路–網格組合都驗證到 1e-15。要注意：在這個模型裡感測器和目標共用同一組誤差，所以「轉移失敗」不可能發生——這是理想化的。
