Value of information

Proposition D1 · value-of-information decomposition

G · action-loss reduction

What switching away from issuing gains, on average.

K · allocation cost

The price of having set aside sensing and reserve shots. Always ≥ 0.

Positive value needs G > K: the evidence must change actions on enough units to pay for the shots it costs.

D1：ΔV 等於 G 減 K。G 是切換動作帶來的期望損失減少，K 是預留感測和修正 shots 的配置成本，永遠非負。要有正的價值，G 必須大於 K。這是整個理論的結論性條件。
