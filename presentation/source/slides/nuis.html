Sensors and identifiability

Nuisance classes

N-free

Arbitrary response maps Rc, closed under translation and small positivity-preserving bumps (continuous and Cᵏ maps qualify).

N-loc

unknown base law q and directions D

N-q

As N-loc, with the directions D known.

N-q ⊆ N-loc, but N-q ⊄ N-free (an affine response leaves the simplex for large angles): the classes are not nested.

三種干擾參數類別：N-free 最寬，回應函數任意；N-loc 是一階線性但基底和方向未知；N-q 是方向已知。關鍵觀察：φ=0 時每個標籤的分佈都是 q + εv，ε 和 q 糾纏在一起。
