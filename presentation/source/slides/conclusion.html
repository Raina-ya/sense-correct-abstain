Conclusion

The evidence must change the optimal action on enough units to pay for the shots it costs. Here it did not.

Labels could not identify ε at φ = 0; a known-reference sensor could, but its rule did not select beneficiaries; always correcting was already near-optimal, so the adaptive policy showed no detectable value. On hardware the sensor was imprecise at the frozen budget and biased.

結論：理論給出條件——證據必須在足夠多的單位上改變最佳動作，多到足以支付它花的 shots。在這個設定下沒有達到。這些結論只對合成模型和凍結設計成立；在真機上證據更弱。
