Three-action theory

Three-action theory

Setting and assumptions

S1 · Evidence

X contains everything the policy may use; δ₀ and the correction estimate are measurable functions of X.

S2 · Policy

A measurable map from X to a distribution over {N, C, A}, with private randomisation U independent of (ϑ, X, Y).

S3 · Stage 2 as a potential outcome

Y = (F, δC) exists on every path and is observed only on C; under D3 the stage-1 allocation does not depend on the action.

ρ = failure probability; c = probability of a wrong corrected answer. All results are analytic and sensor-independent.

理論設定：三個假設。S3 把第二階段（修正重跑）當成潛在結果，在每條路徑上都有定義但只有選 C 時觀察到。這讓我們能寫出條件損失 l_N, l_A, l_C。理論完全不依賴哪種感測器。
