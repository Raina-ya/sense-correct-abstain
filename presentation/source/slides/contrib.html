What the paper adds

1 · Decision theory

A three-action risk identity that reduces to known alarm and reject rules; a blindness lemma with product-law lower bounds; a value-of-information decomposition.

2 · Identifiability

For retained RC labels: zero efficient information, local and global non-identification kept apart; identified sets per nuisance class; a global gap-shift bound.

3 · A documented negative result

A sensor that identifies the error yields neither a selective rule nor a policy that beats always-correct — plus a first hardware pilot of the sensor.

三個貢獻：理論、可識別性分析、以及一個有完整失敗分析的負面結果加上第一個硬體 pilot。
