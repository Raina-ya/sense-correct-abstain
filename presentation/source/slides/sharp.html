Sensors and identifiability

Bounded, but not a point

Proposition · sharp identified set under N-q

If some vc ≠ 0, both ends are finite: ε is locally but not globally non-identified.

Corollary · profile maximisers

At exact probabilities the profile log-likelihood maximisers are exactly I (Gibbs’ inequality), even under misspecification. Hence at φ₀ = 0 the profile maximiser is non-unique in every class.

So the three notions really differ: under N-q the information is zero and identification fails locally, yet I is bounded.

N-q 下的識別集是有界區間，給出明確端點。所以三個概念在這裡真的不同：資訊為零、局部不可識別、但全域可以有界。Gibbs 推論把它連到 Gate 1A：profile 最大概似的解就是整個識別集，不唯一。
