Why the gates came out this way

Why Gate 3 is null

With always-correct selected, the policy adds value only where it deviates from C and the deviation lowers loss.

Always-correct is already near-optimal (0.0079 vs 0.029), so G is bounded by small differences on the 27% of units where the policy deviates — the empirical counterpart of P3.

Offered as an explanation, not as a tested finding.

Why Gate 2 is not selectivity

Balanced accuracy against a constant baseline rewards any statistic associated with the label. The gap-bound condition removes large-gap units where accuracy is already about one.

Labels are also noisy and measure unconditional benefit, not the decision-time quantity Gate 3 scores.

失敗分析。Gate 3 為什麼是零：總是修正已經接近最佳，所以策略能偏離的空間很小，這是 P3 的經驗對應。我們把它當作解釋而不是檢驗過的發現。Gate 2 為什麼不是選擇性：平衡準確率會獎勵任何跟標籤相關的統計量。
