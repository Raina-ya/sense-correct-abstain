Limits of the evidence

Limitations

  • synthetic error model; 2–6 qubits, QAOA depth one, shot noise only
  • P-amp resource condition fails below visibility 0.99263
  • Gate 2 lacked an uncertainty bound and power analysis; Gate 3 underpowered
  • analysis code outside the locks; Monte Carlo selection probabilities
  • post-lock: Gate 3 estimand correction, Gate 2 diagnostics, hardware precision
  • hardware: injected errors, one processor, one pair, one window, eight blocks

What the negative results do not say

Gate 1A does not say RC labels are uninformative: with a known response the oracle information is positive.

Gate 3 does not say adaptive control is useless: with always-correct near-optimal and power 0.0004, the design could not have detected a small gain.

限制和不該誤讀的地方：負面結果有明確的範圍。Gate 1A 不是說標籤沒用，Gate 3 不是說自適應控制沒用——只是在這個設定下沒有證據。
