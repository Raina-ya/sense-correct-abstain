Tensorisation

BC multiplies and KL adds over independent shots; TV does not. “Single-shot TV × n” is valid but quickly vacuous — not a distance at budget.

Upper bound and sample size

常數與方向：BC 在獨立 shots 上相乘，KL 相加，TV 不能這樣做。所以不能把單次 TV 乘以 n 當成預算下的距離。如果要兩個錯誤機率之和小於 β，所需樣本數至少是 log(1/2β) 除以 KL。
