This is a simulation-first research record about fixed-budget decision control under angle-dependent coherent bias in QAOA. It contains analytic work and synthetic simulations only. It contains no hardware-derived evidence and makes no claim that the studied method is operationally effective.

The frozen route was Gate 1A FAIL, Gate 1B PASS with a 0.7% visibility margin, Gate 2 formal PASS with a narrowed practical interpretation, and Gate 3 FAIL under both the frozen and corrected estimands. The project therefore stopped after M6 and proceeded to MT7 synthesis. Read `docs/errata/`, `docs/claim_ledger.md`, and `docs/results/project_status.md` before citing individual gate conclusions.

The repository includes negative and low-power findings, post-lock corrections, explicit generalization boundaries, and platform-dependent floating-point differences. Internal recomputation is not independent or third-party validation.
